3️⃣ Draft-first, pressure-free journaling
Problem

Publishing feels like a “moment” — some users may not be ready.

Improvements

Everything starts as a draft (even mood logs)

Show subtle status chips: Draft • Private • Shared

Add “You can come back to this later” microcopy

Auto-save indicator (“Saved just now”)

You already have the backend structure to support this — it’s mostly UI signaling.

Level it up:

substance

emotional intensity

5️⃣ Sharing & friends: trust signaling is critical

The friend system is good — but users need emotional safety cues.

Improvements

When sharing, explicitly show:

Who can see it

What they can’t do (edit, reshare, etc.)

Add a short line:

“Shared reports are read-only and never indexed or public.”

Add share expiration (7 days / 30 days / forever).
This is huge for trust.
